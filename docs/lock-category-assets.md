# Lock Category Assets

## Pham Vi

5 nhom da duoc duyet: Khoa dien tu, Khoa cua, Khoa tu, Tay nam cua, Phu kien cua.
Moi nhom dung `CategoryProductSection`: banner tren, mot hang 5 `ProductCard` ben duoi,
nut Xem tat ca. Desktop 5 cot, tablet/mobile cuon ngang, khong tran ngang toan trang.
Banner va nut lien ket `/category/khoa?subcategory=<ten nhom>`.
Trang category giu nguyen bo cuc khong banner.

Sua nhom/thu tu/SKU trong `lib/lock-catalog.ts`; sua card chung trong `components/product-card.tsx`.
Banner dung anh cua SKU dau nhom. Anh la bitmap san pham that, khong phai screenshot giao dien.
Tat ca anh luu local; khong goi API HafeleHome khi website chay.
